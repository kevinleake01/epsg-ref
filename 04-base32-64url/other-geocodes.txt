-----------------------------------------------------
----- OTHER GEOCODES USING BASE32 AND BASE64URL -----
-----------------------------------------------------

Here are some examples of using other geocodes converted to
Base32 and Base64url to make them URL and filename safe:

----------------------------------------------------
--- MAPCODES ---

Mapcode:  GBR 762.0HW

Mapcode-Base32:  I5BFEIBXGYZC4MCIK4======
Mapcode-Base64url:  R0JSIDc2Mi4wSFc


Mapcode:  GBR 762.0HW-BTL2

Mapcode-Base32:  I5BFEIBXGYZC4MCIK4WUEVCMGI======
Mapcode-Base64url:  R0JSIDc2Mi4wSFctQlRMMg

-----------------------------------------------------
--- MGRS ---

MGRS:  30U YC 08215 07225

MGRS-Base32:  GMYFKICZIMQDAOBSGE2SAMBXGIZDK===
MGRS-Base64url:  MzBVIFlDIDA4MjE1IDA3MjI1

Note: The MGRS version without spaces (30UYC0821507225) is URL and filename safe.

-----------------------------------------------------
--- PLUS CODES (OLC) ---

OLC:  9C2WWGQG+8W

OLC-Base32:  HFBTEV2XI5IUOKZYK4======
OLC-Base64url:  OUMyV1dHUUcrOFc

Note: You can also make Plus Codes URL and filename safe by changing the Plus '+'
to Minus '-', similar to converting base64 to Base64url. (Example: 9C2WWGQG-8W)

-----------------------------------------------------
--- H3 ---

H3:  8f195911b866064

H3-Base32:  HBTDCOJVHEYTCYRYGY3DANRU
H3-Base64url:  OGYxOTU5MTFiODY2MDY0

Note: H3 only uses Hex numbers, so it is URL and filename safe.
